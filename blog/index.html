Writing

I started writing about software and publishing it on the internet in 2016. My writing then was largely focused on programming craft, golang internals and infrastructure — foobarcat.blogspot.com.

Now my focus is primarily mechanism level analysis and intervention in systems where software, organisations, and psychology intersect. I also use this space to share context and provide updates on ongoing projects.

If you are new to my writing then it is suggested that you start here:
